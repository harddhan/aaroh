# AAROH

### AI Archive & Research-Oriented Heritage

AAROH is a digital heritage and research platform for exploring the writings, speeches, documents, photographs, historical places, and constitutional contributions of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar. It combines a curated archival database with AI-assisted research, semantic search, interactive timelines, and historical geospatial exploration, designed for researchers, students, cultural institutions, and touchscreen museum experiences.

Built with React, Vite, FastAPI, FAISS, and modern AI retrieval technologies. The project includes a structured archive, interactive heritage map, chronological timeline, AI Research Assistant, and source-aware archival data.
